### Rosamunde C. Hendricks

**PhD student**

Rosamunde is a PhD student in Neuroscience at the Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB). She finalised her Bachelor’s degree in Psychology at the University of Amsterdam (UvA) in 2021. Furthermore, she finished her first Master’s degree in Research in Behavior and Cognition from the University of Barcelona (UB) in 2022, while finalising a second Master’s degree from the Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB) in 2024. Her research is focused on the intersection between social media and neuroscience. Specifically, as part of the project VALAWAI she looks into the moral aspect of AI and social media.
