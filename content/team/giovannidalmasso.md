### Giovanni Dalmasso

**Post-doctoral researcher**

Giovanni Dalmasso is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Social Brain Lab, Hospital del Mar Research Institute in Barcelona. He combines mathematical and computational modeling with psychology and neuroscience to study human moral decision-making. His work also includes tissue biology and disease modeling, focusing on organogenesis and vascular formation. Giovanni earned his PhD in Natural Sciences from the University of Heidelberg, researching at DKFZ and BioQuant. He holds a Master’s and Bachelor’s in Mathematical Engineering from the Polytechnic University of Turin, Italy. Currently, as part of the VALAWAI project Giovanni is developing an AI model inspired by moral psychology and contractualism, aiming to create AI systems that respect human autonomy.
