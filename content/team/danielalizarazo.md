### Daniela Lizarazo-Villarreal

**PhD student**

Daniela is a PhD student in Neuroscience at the Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB). She received her Bachelor’s in Psychology and Language and Sociocultural Studies at Universidad de los Andes in 2019 and her Master’s degree in Cognitive Neuroscience at Sapienza University of Rome in 2023. Her research focuses on social neuroscience, misinformation and politics.
